How to use this calculator

Same logic as the Excel workbook. Follow these steps, then explore Parameters, Measurement and Allocations.

1
Open Parameters

Enter your Elastic Cloud rates (ECU/h), snapshot monthly costs, hours/month and annual ECU commit. Yellow fields are editable.

2
Fill Measurement

Add one row per index family or data-stream pattern with GB hot, GB frozen and an environment code from Parameters. Or import a CSV.

3
Read Allocations

See the ECU/month share per family without admin cost, and with surcharge on environments marked for commit uplift.

4
Group workloads

In Workloads, pick several families for a business owner (e.g. cloud app logs) and get a rolled-up chargeback.

5
Inspect one family

Any family walks through the same 16 calculation steps as the Excel sheet — useful for audits and explanations.

Tips

  • Your edits are saved in the browser (localStorage). Export CSV before clearing cache.
  • Events/s and KB/doc are context only — they do not drive the ECU share.
  • Sample names and volumes are fictional. Never paste real customer inventories into a public fork.

Allocation formula (simplified)

family_ECU/month ≈ (GB_hot_family / GB_hot_env) × ECU/h_hot × hours + (GB_frozen_family / GB_frozen_env) × (ECU/h_frozen × hours + snapshots/month) + optional surcharge if env is marked

Parameters

Global contract figures. Environments below are fully configurable — add DEV, INT, UAT, PROD, MONITORING or any codes your org uses.

Environments (clusters)

Each row is one billable environment / cluster. Tick “Surcharge” for workloads that share the annual commit uplift. Untick for dedicated clusters (e.g. monitoring) billed at their own rate.
